## OLIST BUSINESS QUESTIONS — EXECUTIVE LEVEL

#### REVENUE & GROWTH
1. What is total revenue by month? Is it growing?
2. What is average order value? Is it increasing?
3. Which product categories generate the most revenue?

#### CUSTOMERS
4. How many new vs returning customers each month?
5. Which states have the most customers?
6. What is the repeat purchase rate?

#### SELLERS
7. Which sellers generate the most revenue?
8. Which sellers have the worst delivery performance?
9. How many active sellers per month?

#### OPERATIONS & DELIVERY
10. What is the average delivery time (purchase to delivery)?
11. What % of orders are delivered late vs on time?
12. Which states have the worst delivery times?

#### CUSTOMER SATISFACTION
13. What is the average review score overall?
14. Which product categories get the worst reviews?
15. Is there a link between late delivery and low review scores?
